## 👋 About Me

Hi, I'm **Love Mishra** --- a B.Tech student in **Internet of Things
(IoT)** at **Madhav Institute of Technology & Science**.

I'm interested in building practical products at the intersection of
**full-stack development, connected devices, and real-world problem
solving**. I enjoy taking an idea from concept to prototype, learning
new tools, and collaborating with people who like building things.

-   💻 Focused on **full-stack web development**
-   🌱 Exploring modern JavaScript development and scalable application
    design
-   🌾 Building **AgroPulse**, an IoT-based agricultural sensing project
-   🧠 Practising problem-solving and strengthening my programming
    fundamentals
-   🤝 Open to hackathons, collaborations, internships, and meaningful
    projects
-   📍 Based in India
